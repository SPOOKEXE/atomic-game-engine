| Route | Native controls | Source behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extends | path, side, length | Constructor metadata is computed with initial side/length zero before requested controls are assigned. The end endpoint is sampled at .999. Boundary and accumulated-length quirks are preserved. Signed length remains signed. |
| Flattern | path, reverse, ping_pong | Retains original child boundaries. Each child line repeats its original line-zero length/segment metadata. Sampling forwards the global flattened index to the original child. Missing selection falls back to its last child at total length and line zero. Boundary remains the source -4 sentinel. |

Zero or negative active steps produce undefined source arithmetic and diagnose; samples outside range still bypass smoothing. |
