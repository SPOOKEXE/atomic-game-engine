# Clean

Every character an em dash gets confused with, and not one em dash.

- A hyphen in a compound word: first-party, hand-edited, top-level.
- A double hyphen as a flag: `--verbose`, `--frames 100`.
- An en dash spanning a range: pages 10–14, the years 2014–2018.
- A minus sign in front of a number: −1, −0.5, −273.15.
- An arrow and a bullet, which are neither: → and ·.

Reporting any line of this file means the check matches more than U+2014.
